    Sign in, accept a firm quote and prove eligibility. The mock bank records the TRY receipt; the vault releases the exact mock USDC to your wallet.

    Mock USDC to simulated TRY

    WHAT ZKPASSPORT ADDS

    Proof instead of document collection

    A native Soroban verifier checks age and country predicates for the exact order. The bank receipt is a separate attestation. Neither a phone callback nor a backend flag unlocks tokens.
