    How this demo works

    Deposit: simulated TRY in, mock USDC to your wallet. Withdraw: mock USDC in, simulated TRY to a made-up bank reference.

    ZKPassport proves age, nationality and document-issuer requirements. The Soroban vault checks eligibility and bank receipts before releasing tokens.

    Simulated bank / ZKPassport / Testnet wallet

    Wallet, amount, order, policy and submitted proof transactions are public. Country is nationality or document issuer, not residence. Synthetic documents only; this is not production identity or compliance verification.

    Built on Kaan's TR Mock Anchor. SEP-10 login and SEP-38 quotes with custom settlement, not full SEP-6 conformance. Experimental and not independently audited.
